# SURF project timeline

A concise, chronological path through the whole summer, phase by phase. This
complements `docs/project_overview/PROJECT_STORY.md` (the full narrative
prose) and the per-study READMEs under `docs/studies/` (the deep-dive
detail, including what didn't work) — read this page first for orientation,
then follow the links for depth. Dates are first-commit dates on the branch
that produced each phase's work, not calendar deadlines.

| Phase | Dates | Channel / dataset | Main question | Models / tools | Result | Why the project moved on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. HH→bbWW baseline | 2026-05 → 06-24 | HH→bbWW, COLLIDE-1M | Can b-jet regression + jet-pairing + a tabular/LBN classifier separate HH→bbWW from ttbar/Z→bb? | BDT, tabular DNN, 4 LBN variants, first SPA-Net-for-Hbb attempt | Best LBN (obj+aux) ≈ BDT reference (Z≈0.0066 vs 0.0068); first SPA-Net attempt underperformed the pair-classifier baseline (0.55 vs 0.70 purity) | See phase 2 — study [`01_hh_bbww`](../studies/01_hh_bbww/README.md) |

| 5. Classical ML baselines | 2026-07-13 → 08-11 | HH→4b, same production | How far do cut / BDT / DNN / LBN baselines get before a symmetry-aware model is needed? | rectangular cut scan, BDT v1/v2/v3, tabular DNN, LBN-DNN, a 1000-replica selection-stability bootstrap | Model hierarchy BDT > DNN > LBN; primary BDT (`global_v1_mass_aware`) AUC=0.7737; optimized cut ≈ historical simple cut; **validation campaign blocked by infrastructure failure**, honestly reported as a non-result | Motivated a symmetry-aware architecture — study [`04_hh4b_classical_ml`](../studies/04_hh4b_classical_ml/README.md) |
| 6. | 7. Training-scale and tail reliability | 2026-08-21 → 09-07 | HH→4b, matched cohort | Does 5× more training data (10M vs 2M) help, and can the governing checkpoint's rare-event tail be trusted numerically and statistically? | SPA-Net 10M, FP32 score census, causal 5th-jet masking, QCD tail-stability checks | Classification **saturated** (AUC 0.969272 at 10M vs 0.969281 at 2M); FP32 quantization found kinematically unremarkable; QCD tail stable at u>3.5, too few events to judge above u>4.5 | Cleared the governing checkpoint for use as the baseline in the ParT/ZERO20 comparison — study [`06_scaling_and_tail_reliability`](../studies/06_scaling_and_tail_reliability/README.md) |
| 8. Pretrained ParT representation | 2026-09-08 → 09-11 | HH→4b, matched cohort | Does appending a frozen, pretrained (JetClass-**supervised**, not self-supervised) Particle Transformer embedding to SPA-Net's native input help? | SPA-Net + 20 variance-selected frozen ParT dims ("active20") | **Significant harm**: ΔAUC −0.0251, Δ(exact-event reconstruction) −0.371; root-cause audit narrowed the cause to optimization dynamics + a lossy variance-only feature-selection rule | Motivated a controlled ablation to isolate the cause — study [`07_pretrained_jet_representations`](../studies/07_pretrained_jet_representations/README.md) |
| 9. ZERO20 width-control ablation | 2026-09-14 | HH→4b, matched cohort | Is the active20 harm caused by the wider (7→27) input layer alone, or by the specific ParT content? | SPA-Net + 20 constant-zero dims, identical architecture/training contract | **Native-like** on both metrics (ΔAUC −0.00017, ~30× below the pre-registered practical floor; reconstruction statistically indistinguishable) — falsifies "input width alone" | Rules out one of two candidate mechanisms; points at ParT content/preprocessing specifically — same study, [`07_pretrained_jet_representations`](../studies/07_pretrained_jet_representations/README.md) |
| 10. Full128/full144 scale-feasibility and representation-learning direction | 2026-08-26 → present | HH→4b, 144M-event population | Is scaling the ParT+SPA-Net comparison to 10M or 144M events feasible, and what would a JP-JEPA (self-supervised) alternative representation need? | feasibility audit (no production launched); JP-JEPA compatibility-prep (CPU/GPU numerics) | Feasibility: recommended next step is 10M scale, not full144, pending resolution of the ParT checkpoint's still-open domain-mismatch verdict; JP-JEPA: exploratory compatibility groundwork only, explicitly not a production authorization | **Future work**, not a current result — see `docs/studies/07_pretrained_jet_representations/full144_scale_feasibility_20260826.md` and `docs/checkpoints/track_g_jpjepa_spanet_compatibility_prep_20260908_v1/` |

## Notes on this timeline

- This table intentionally does not claim every phase uses the same dataset
  or evaluation contract — see `docs/results/model_landscape.md` for the
  explicit per-model dataset/contract columns, and do not read this table's
  "Result" column as a cross-phase ranking.
- Several large, internally rigorous campaigns (the 41-checkpoint physical
  normalization freeze; the 1000-replica cut-baseline selection-stability
  study) are compressed to one timeline row each — see the linked study
  README and `docs/provenance/REPOSITORY_CONTENT_MAP.md` for the full
  campaign breakdown.
- Branches with unique work not reflected in this timeline (the original
  pivot-decision document, a draft paper manuscript, two later unmerged
  studies) are catalogued in [`BRANCH_GUIDE.md`](BRANCH_GUIDE.md), not here.
