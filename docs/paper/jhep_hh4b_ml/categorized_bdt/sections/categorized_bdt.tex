\section{Train-only nested categorized BDT}
This HIG-24-015-inspired study is based only on Delphes simulation and frozen train-source-group folds. No validation, test, observed-data, or Run-2 expected-sensitivity claim is made. The primary multijet projection is exactly $3b\rightarrow{\geq4b}$; direct $\geq4b$ QCD is secondary closure only.

Mass-aware categorized BDT selected category counts (fold 0: 6, fold 1: 6, fold 2: 6, fold 3: 6, fold 4: 6). Its joint nominal sensitivity is $0.0695^{+0.0081}_{-0.0067}$ and its systematics-aware sensitivity is $(5.84^{+0.96}_{-0.82})\times10^{-2}$; the selected $S/B$ is $(1.91^{+0.18}_{-0.15})\times10^{-5}$.

Mass-plane-blind categorized BDT selected category counts (fold 0: 6, fold 1: 6, fold 2: 6, fold 3: 6, fold 4: 6). Its joint nominal sensitivity is $0.0695^{+0.0066}_{-0.0051}$ and its systematics-aware sensitivity is $(5.92^{+0.74}_{-0.60})\times10^{-2}$; the selected $S/B$ is $(1.91^{+0.18}_{-0.15})\times10^{-5}$.

For Mass-aware selected categorized BDT minus inclusive BDT, the paired nominal-$Z_A$ difference is $0.0057^{+0.0061}_{-0.0048}$ and excludes zero at the 68\% interval.

For Mass-plane-blind selected categorized BDT minus inclusive BDT, the paired nominal-$Z_A$ difference is $0.0060^{+0.0062}_{-0.0068}$ and includes zero, so no improvement is claimed.

All resampleable values use the same common source-member bootstrap registry. Primary uncertainties are asymmetric 16th--84th percentile intervals. Selection stability is reported separately from evaluation uncertainty, and a single correlated multijet nuisance is profiled jointly across exclusive categories.
