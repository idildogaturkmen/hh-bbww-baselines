# Track B Provenance Gaps

Bounded benchmark-readiness audit, 2026-08-13. This lists every point where a claim
could NOT be closed to full satisfaction, plus one correction to a prior in-tree
artifact's citation label.

---

## 1. Citation-label correction (not a factual error, but flagged for hygiene)

`phase4G_transfer_and_roc_protocol_freeze/PHASE4G_PROTOCOL.md` and
`phase4G_protocol.json` cite the training/inference-QCD orthogonality statement as
coming from **"Appendix C.1.1"** of arXiv:2508.15048v2.

This audit fetched the actual paper PDF (arxiv.org/pdf/2508.15048, verified
2026-08-13) and read the Supplementary Material table of contents and body text
directly. The paper's own section numbering is **Appendix C, subsection 1
("Jet-free model development"), sub-subsection a ("Training samples and label
definitions")** -- i.e. "C.1.a", not "C.1.1". The content of the citation is
CONFIRMED CORRECT; only the section-label notation differs. Verbatim text, p.10:

> "These samples are generated following the same procedures used for the
> inference samples described in Appendix B.1, while remaining statistically
> orthogonal to the datasets used for model inference."

Raw generated QCD events for training stated in the paper: 2.0e11. Raw generated
ttbar: 4.0e7. Post-trigger training QCD: 6.3e7 (63M, matches the distributed
62,941,859 almost exactly). Post-trigger training ttbar: 1.2e7 (12M, matches the
distributed 11,599,999 almost exactly). This is genuine author documentation, not
a fabricated claim -- the prior Phase-4G artifact was right to treat it as
author-documented; this audit independently re-verified it against the primary
source rather than accepting the citation on faith.

**This does not, however, resolve event-ID-level independence** (see item 2).

## 2. QCD training/inference independence: two distinct claims, only one resolved

- **Generation-provenance-level claim (RESOLVED, author-documented):** the paper's
  Supplementary Material explicitly states training QCD is generated by a separate
  campaign from the campaign(s) used for inference QCD, "while remaining
  statistically orthogonal." This is a genuine, verified author statement.
- **Event-identity-level claim (STILL UNRESOLVED):** the distributed ntuples for
  training_qcd, inference_qcd_1, inference_qcd_2 carry NO run/lumi/job/event-ID
  branches (87-branch schema, confirmed identical across all three QCD lanes; see
  `phase4C3_generation_weight_tag_contract/evidence/representative_branch_set_differences.tsv`).
  It is therefore not possible to cross-check for accidental event duplication or
  overlap directly from the released data. Zero exact-path and zero basename
  overlap were confirmed at the file level (`phase4B_remote_dataset_inventory_r2/evidence/qcd_path_overlap_audit.tsv`),
  but file-naming disjointness does not, by itself, prove event-level statistical
  independence.

Net position carried into the readiness report: **QCD_STATISTICAL_INDEPENDENCE is
author-documented at the generation-provenance level (verified against the primary
source in this audit) but not independently event-ID-verifiable from the
distributed data.** This is treated as a qualified/partial resolution, not a full
UNRESOLVED, and not a full RESOLVED=true in the strict event-ID sense.

## 3. Training-QCD physical weight: still UNRESOLVED, now with a partial reconciliation

The author `weight_dict` QCD formula denominator term `(17600+38072)*5,000,000 =
2.7836e11` was compared in this audit against the paper's own stated raw
generated-QCD-for-training count of `2.0e11` (Supp. Mat. p.10). These are the same
order of magnitude but differ by ~28% -- not an exact match. This means the
`17600`/`38072` job-count terms in the published `weight_dict["QCD"]` formula most
likely correspond to the two **inference** QCD production campaigns (consistent
with Phase-4C3's original `STRONG_CANDIDATE_COMBINED_INFERENCE_QCD` finding), not
the training-QCD campaign, whose own raw-generated count (2.0e11) does not appear
as an exact factor anywhere in the released `weight_dict`.

**`TRAINING_QCD_WEIGHT_CONTRACT_RESOLVED=false` is retained.** No physical
normalization is applied to training_qcd anywhere in this audit's outputs.

## 4. Mass-plane SR/CR analog: constructible, but explicitly a simplified analog

The Phase-4G bounded canary in this audit used:
- a deterministic minimum-|m1-m2| pairing over the three 2+2 combinations of the
  4 tight-tagged jets (no ML/tagger-score input to the pairing itself), and
- a circular window centered at (125,125) GeV with R_HH<30 / 30<=R_HH<55, as
  specified in the governing task instructions.

The paper's own **uncorrected cut-based** resolved-channel reproduction (the most
directly comparable "kinematics-only, no ML mass regression" baseline in the
paper) instead uses a diagonal-distance pairing `d_HH=|m_H1-1.04*m_H2|/sqrt(1+1.04^2)`
and centers its window at **(110,105) GeV, radius 25 GeV** -- shifted low relative
to the true 125 GeV Higgs mass specifically because no b-jet energy regression is
applied (Supp. Mat. Appendix B.2.a, p.7). Track B, like that baseline, has no
b-jet energy regression available either, so a MORE faithful "uncorrected
kinematic analog" would plausibly re-center near (110,105)/r=25 rather than
(125,125)/R<30. This was not re-run in this bounded audit (out of scope per the
governing instructions, which specified R_HH<30/30-55 explicitly); it is recorded
here as a refinement for any follow-up that wants to track the paper's own
baseline more closely. The (125,125) GeV point itself is not arbitrary -- it is
the paper's own truth Higgs mass point / ML mass-window center (Supp. Mat.
Appendix C.4, Fig. S6) -- but is used here on RAW kinematic pairing rather than
their ML-corrected mass estimate, per the explicit instruction not to use
jet-free-model output to define the region.

## 5. SPA-Net truth-assignment labels: not directly stored

See `TRACKB_MODEL_INPUT_READINESS.tsv`. The flat ntuples store `gen_bhadron_fromhh`
as a boolean only (no h1-vs-h2 index) and `gen_higgs1_*`/`gen_higgs2_*` as
aggregate truth Higgs 4-vectors, but no direct per-jet Higgs-parent index branch.
A deterministic deltaR/mass-based combinatorial matching adapter is required and
was not built or validated in this audit (design only, per the governing
"do not train" instruction).

## 6. JP-JEPA input contract: not independently confirmed

Track B's constituent branches (part_px/py/pz/energy/charge/pid/d0/dz/label) are
generically sufficient for a JEPA-style particle encoder, matching what ParT needs.
Whether they satisfy the SPECIFIC input contract of "JP-JEPA" could not be checked
in this audit because JP-JEPA's architecture/feature specification is not part of
the jetfree-hh4b repository or its associated paper -- it is presumed to be a
Track-A-side or external artifact. This is flagged as an open action item, not a
blocker discovered in Track B itself.
