# HH→4b CMS sensitivity-gap update for Harvey v3

## Computed facts

The pooled `R_HH<34` expected limit, μ95=74.015, is independently validated by `1.96/ZA=74.010`. Keeping exact3/ge4 separate gives 44.328 (independent approximation 44.311), proving that category structure contains real information. CMS HIG-24-010 remains the closest benchmark at expected μ95=5.9, but uses collision data, full detector reconstruction, control regions, and a data-driven multibin background model.

Physical selected QCD is 46.45 million events from 123 weighted rows (Neff=52.4). The dominant pTHat strata are 75–100, 100–200, and 50–75 GeV. Only six physical-QCD rows populate ge4 `R_HH<34`; every populated pTHat×RHH cell has Neff=1 and `[30,34)` is empty.

## Validated diagnostics

The 2b→3b transfer passes the frozen closure gate: overall yield ratio 1.005, held-fold ratios 0.821 and 1.151, maximum shape TV 0.146, and target Neff 117.7. An exact3-only transfer-constrained diagnostic propagating the worst held-fold deviation (17.86%) gives μ95=115,372, compared with exact3 direct-MC Gaussian 91,074 and stat-only 98.39. These huge values are diagnostics: the transfer is validated as a closure method but is not yet a competitive constraint.

## Unsupported or optimistic diagnostics

The shape μ95=31.96 is optimistic and not a defensible physics sensitivity. Deterministic support-only merging retains only 5.0%, 4.3%, and approximately 0% of its nominal gain for QCD-Neff thresholds 5, 10, and 20. No threshold is achievable in every ge4 bin.

No ge4 transfer is validated. Direct 2b→ge4 gives 0.294 predicted/target. The new 3b→ge4 test gives 4.43 overall and changes from 0.94 in fold 3 to 5.24 in fold 4. Sequential 2b→3b→ge4 and joint {2b,3b}→ge4 remain near 0.294. The target has 24 rows and Neff=12.1. Shape TVs reach 0.719 for 3b→ge4 and 0.485 for the sequential RHH closure.

## Next remediation

The obsolete bottleneck is not absence of lower-tag physical events; 1.04 million such train rows exist. The bottleneck is the lack of a statistically reliable high-tag QCD prediction/transfer constraint, especially in the ge4 signal-like tail.

Evidence supports both continued transfer development and targeted new QCD generation, but only 2b→3b is currently usable. A targeted heavy-flavor/tail-stratified QCD design is likely required; its filter/overlap protocol and CPU pilot are not ready, so no production is authorized. The pending global BDT will test recoverable discrimination, but its highest-score bins must pass event/source/Neff and lower-tag score-closure audits before any sensitivity gain is accepted.
