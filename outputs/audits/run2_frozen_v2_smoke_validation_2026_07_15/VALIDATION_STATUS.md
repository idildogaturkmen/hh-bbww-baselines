# Validation status: passed for the intended fast-simulation scope

The frozen-v2 card passed statistical smoke validation.

The observed AK4 tagging efficiencies for b, c, and light-flavor jets are
consistent with the efficiency formulas encoded in the Delphes card across the
populated jet-pT bins.

Event-by-event identity between independent Delphes runs is not required
because detector smearing, reconstruction efficiencies, and b tagging are
stochastic. Consequently, individual jet and tag migration between the old and
new runs must not be interpreted solely as an effect of changing the flavor
association radius.

The card is frozen for a 13 TeV Run-2-like Delphes fast-simulation study. It is
not an exact reproduction of the CMS detector, DeepJet, ParticleNet, pileup, or
trigger response.
