# Status: superseded normalization prototype

These tables are retained for provenance but must not be quoted as final
analysis yields.

The signal uses approximately correct 13 TeV SM normalization, but the listed
candidate efficiencies correspond only to the current AK4/AK8 preselection.

The QCD bbbb, Zbbbb, ttbar, ZZ, and ZH rows do not represent the final CMS-style
background model. The final analysis will use a three-b-tag/four-b-tag
control-region transfer-factor method for the dominant multijet background,
with theory-normalized simulation used for optimization, closure tests, and
minor-background estimates.
